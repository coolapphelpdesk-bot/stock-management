Privacy Policy

Stock Management · last updated November 23, 2026

1. Summary

Stock Management (“the app”) is an inventory-management app with on-device barcode scanning, published by TeamCoolApps.

  • The developer collects no personal data from the app and operates no servers that the app talks to. There are no developer accounts.
  • Your inventory stays on your device. It leaves the device only when you send it somewhere: a backup to a destination you set up (a folder, your own FTP or SMB server, or your own Google Drive), a report you export or share, or a browser you let in through Remote Access on your local network.
  • Barcode recognition runs on-device via Google ML Kit; camera frames and decoded barcode values are never transmitted by the app.
  • The app contains no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect personal data, telemetry, identifiers, device information, IP addresses, advertising IDs, or usage analytics, and never receives your inventory, your backups or your Google account details.

News sign-up on our website (optional, separate from the app). If you choose to leave your email address on coolapp-stock-management.web.app/news/ — the app’s Support and What’s new screens link to it — we keep that address, the language of the page, whether you offered to test new versions, and the date you signed up. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase, EU region) and used only to email you news about the app and, if you asked, the link to join its testers on Google Play; a tester’s address is also added to the app’s tester list in Google Play Console. It is never sold or shared with anyone else. Nothing is sent from the app itself: the form is a web page opened in your browser.

4. Google Drive backups (optional)

If you add Google Drive as a backup destination, you sign in with your own Google account through Google Play services, and the app asks for one permission only: drive.file. With it the app can see and change only the files it created itself — a “Stock Management” folder and the backup files in it. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • What is sent: your backup files, uploaded from your device straight to your own Drive. They never pass through the developer, who has no access to your Drive or your backups.
  • What the app reads from Google: your account’s email address (to label the destination) and the list of backup files in its own folder (to show and restore them).
  • What is kept on the device: the account email and the folder’s id. Sign-in tokens are held by Google Play services, not by the app.
  • Removing access: deleting the Drive destination in the app revokes the app’s access; you can also remove it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Stock Management’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the backup feature you set up; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not read by any person.

5. Other backups, exports and Remote Access

  • Backups to a folder, FTP/FTPS or SMB server. Sent only to the folder or server you enter. The developer never receives them.
  • Exported reports, bills, labels and catalogues. Saved or shared only where you choose.
  • Remote Access (off by default). When you switch it on, the app runs a small web server on your phone, reachable only from private local-network addresses (your Wi-Fi or your phone’s hotspot), so other devices you allow can use the app in a browser. Browsers must sign in as a user you created, and the app keeps an activity log of sign-ins and changes on the device. Nothing is reachable from the internet and nothing is sent to the developer.

7. Third-party services

Google ML Kit (barcode scanning). Recognition runs entirely on-device — camera frames and decoded barcode values are never sent to Google or to the developer. Google Play services may download or update the on-device barcode model in the background; that is managed by Google Play services, not by the app.

Google Play (in-app reviews and updates). The app uses Google Play’s own libraries to offer a rating card and to install app updates. These are handled by the Google Play Store app on your device.

Google Drive — only if you set it up; see section 4.

Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app package includes this SDK, but it is not started before 1 January 2028: until then it requests no ads and sends nothing. This policy will be updated before that changes.

These services are governed by Google’s privacy policy. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, social or attribution SDKs.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for business and productivity use and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data in the app remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Stock Management → Storage, or uninstall the app. Backups you made stay wherever you sent them (a folder, your server, your Google Drive) until you delete them there.

An address left on the website’s news sign-up stays on the list until you ask for it to be removed: write to coolapphelpdesk@gmail.com and it is deleted from the list (and from the tester list, if it was on it).

10. Security

Your data is stored in the app’s private database, protected by Android’s application sandboxing. Backup-server passwords are encrypted with a key that never leaves this device’s Android Keystore; Remote Access passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. Uploads to Google Drive use HTTPS. The app is distributed through Google Play, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

11. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
