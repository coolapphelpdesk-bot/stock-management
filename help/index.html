Getting started

What the app is, the first screen, and finding your way around.

What is Stock Management?

An inventory app for shops, warehouses and small businesses. You keep a list of products with SKUs and barcodes, record stock coming in and going out, and always know how much you have, where it is, and what it is worth. It also does stocktakes, sales with printed bills, reports, labels and backups.

Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account to create and no subscription.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works fully offline and has no sign-in. Your data never leaves the device unless you send it somewhere yourself (a backup, an exported report).

Only a few things use a network: sending feedback, opening the Google Play page, backups to an FTP or SMB server, and Remote access (which uses your local Wi-Fi, not the internet).

The first screen asks how I want to start. What should I choose?
  • Restore a backup — you already used the app on another phone and have a backup. See moving to a new phone.
  • Import from a spreadsheet — your products are already in Excel or Google Sheets. See Import.
  • Start with my own inventory — an empty app; add products one by one.
  • A sample set (General store, Clothing shop, Bookstore) — fills the app with demo products so you can look around.

This screen only appears once, but nothing is lost by choosing: you can import, restore or add products at any time later.

How do I load or remove the sample data?

Sample data can only be added while the app is empty: on the welcome screen, or from the Try it with sample data card on the Dashboard. The app tells you exactly how many products, categories, suppliers and locations it will add before it adds them.

To remove it, tap Clear sample data on the Dashboard banner. Only the sample records are removed.

Where are all the screens? I only see four at the bottom.

On a phone the bottom bar shows the Dashboard and the first three screens of your menu. Everything else is in the ⋮ menu at the top right of each screen: Sell, Stocktake, Transactions, Reports, Categories, Suppliers, Locations, Labels, Import, Backup, Trash, Remote access and Settings. On a tablet they are all in the side rail.

To choose which screens get the bottom bar, go to Settings → Menu configuration and move them up or down. You can hide screens you never use too (Dashboard and Settings always stay).

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The app is available in English, Español, Français, Português, Русский, 中文, العربية, हिन्दी, বাংলা, اردو and Bahasa Indonesia.

What does the Dashboard show?

Total products, low stock, stock value, recent moves, alerts and charts (stock levels, products and value by category, movements over 7 and 30 days, what needs reordering, stock value by location and by supplier, most moved products, and lots expiring soon).

Tap Customize dashboard at the foot of the Dashboard, or go to Settings → Dashboard, to hide cards, change their order and set how wide each one is.

Products

Adding, editing, organizing and finding products.

How do I add a product?
  1. Open Products and tap +.
  2. Fill in SKU and Name (both required). The SKU is your own code for the item and must be unique.
  3. Optionally add a photo, description, category, location, supplier, unit (units, kg, litres…), min and max stock, cost price, selling price and barcode. The barcode field has a scan button.
  4. Enter the Quantity you have right now. This is recorded as the product’s initial stock.
  5. Tap Save product.

Faster ways: scan an unknown barcode in the Scanner and tap Add product with this barcode, or import a whole list from a spreadsheet.

Why can’t I change the quantity when I edit a product?

On purpose. Every change of stock is recorded as a movement (who, when, why, before and after), so the history always adds up. Change quantities with Stock in / Stock out, Quick stock, the Scanner or a Stocktake. Everything else on the product (name, prices, barcode, category…) is edited with the pencil on the product page.

How do I add a photo to a product?

On the product form tap Add photo, then Take photo or Choose from gallery. Products without a photo show a parcel picture in their category’s colour.

How do I delete a product, and can I get it back?

Open the product and tap the bin icon at the top. Its past movements stay in the history. The product goes to the Trash, where you can restore it later.

How do I search, sort and filter the product list?

The search box finds products by name, SKU or barcode; the scan button beside it searches by scanning. The sort and filter button sorts by name, quantity, stock value or recently updated, and narrows the list to one category. Tapping Low stock on the Dashboard opens the list showing only low products. On a tablet you can also choose how many columns the list uses.

Can I choose what each product shows in the list?

Yes: Settings → Product card → Customize product card. Show or hide the photo, category, supplier, location, prices, cost, description, stock bar and more, and put them in your own order. A live preview shows the result.

How do categories, suppliers and locations work?

They are lists you file products under. Create them from their own screens in the ⋮ menu (Categories, Suppliers, Locations) with +, or let an import create them. Then pick them on the product form.

Tap any category, supplier or location to see the products filed under it and how much of each is in stock.

Deleting one never deletes products: they are simply left without that category, supplier or location, and restoring it from the Trash links them back.

How does “low stock” work?

Each product has its own Min stock. When the quantity is at or below it, the product is marked low (and out of stock at zero). If it has a Max stock and goes above it, it is marked overstock.

The value pre-filled for new products is set in Settings → Default Low Stock Threshold. Low products show on the Dashboard, in the product list (tap the low stock tile), in the low stock report and in the daily low stock alert.

How do I change the currency?

Settings → General → Currency Symbol. Type the symbol you use (for example $, €, MAD, ₹). It changes how prices are shown; it does not convert amounts.

Stock in & out

Recording what arrives and what leaves.

How do I add or remove stock?
  1. Open the product and tap Adjust stock.
  2. Choose Stock in (a delivery, a return) or Stock out (a sale, damage, use).
  3. Enter the quantity, and a reason if you like (“Order #123”, “Damaged”).
  4. If you use locations, choose where the stock goes in or comes out from.
  5. Confirm. The new quantity shows straight away and the movement is added to Transactions.

The same sheet opens from the product list, from Quick stock and after a scan in the Scanner.

What is Quick stock?

A grid of your products with + and − on each tile: one tap adds or removes one. Handy at a counter, and for items without a barcode (the No barcode filter shows just those). Tap a tile for the full stock sheet.

After a + or −, an undo arrow replaces the price on that tile for a few seconds. Taps made while the list is scrolling are ignored, so you won’t change stock by accident.

How do I set an exact quantity after counting?

Use Stocktake. You type what you counted, and the app works out the difference and records it as an adjustment. That way you never have to do the subtraction yourself.

Why does the app refuse a stock-out, or say there is only a little here?

Stock can’t go below zero. If a product is held in several places, a stock-out takes from the place you chose (or the default location). When that place doesn’t hold enough, the message tells you where the rest is, for example: “Only 3 pcs here. In stock: Store A 5, Van 2”. Choose that place in Stock out from, or move the stock first.

I entered the wrong amount. How do I fix it?

See correcting or undoing a movement.

Locations & transfers

Keeping stock in several places — warehouse, shop, van, shelf — and moving it between them.

How do I move (transfer) stock from one location to another?

1. Create your locations (once): ⋮ → Locations → +, give each one a name and a short code (e.g. Main Warehouse, WH-MN), and save.

2. Move the stock:

  1. Open the product. The Where it is card lists every place that holds some of its stock.
  2. Tap the line for where the stock is now, then Move.
  3. Under To, pick the destination and enter the amount (it shows how much is available).
  4. Confirm. Only that amount moves; the rest stays where it was.

From a location instead: ⋮ → Locations, tap the location, and use Move next to any product held there.

Each transfer appears in Transactions as Transfer (“Warehouse → Store A”) and in the Transfers log report. Made a mistake? Open it in Transactions and tap Revert.

Moving all of a product: you can also edit the product and change its Location. If all its stock is in one place, it moves with it.

I don’t see the “Where it is” card or the Move button.
  • Create at least one location first. The card only appears once there is somewhere to move stock to.
  • Update the app. Moving part of a product’s stock arrived in version 1.79; older versions can only change the location of the whole product.
My stock shows “No location”. How do I put it somewhere?

Stock added before you created locations (or whose location was deleted) is held under No location. Move it like any other stock: open the product, tap the No location line in Where it is, then Move and choose the location.

What is the default location?

The place new stock goes and stock-outs come from when you don’t choose. In Locations, edit a location and switch on Set as default. A product sitting in just one place always uses that place.

How do I choose where a stock-out or a sale takes from?

When a product is held in more than one place or batch, the stock sheet shows Stock out from. Leave it on Automatic (earliest expiry first) or pick the exact place or lot. In Sell, tap Take from: usual place to choose a location for the sale.

Quick stock’s + and − ask where when a product is split. To always use the default location instead, turn off Settings → Stock movements → Ask where, when stock is split.

Can I mark stock as damaged, reserved or in quarantine?

Yes. Move it as above and change the Condition (Good, Damaged, Reserved, Quarantine) instead of, or as well as, the location. It stays counted, but only good stock is sold or taken out automatically.

What happens to the stock if I delete a location?

Nothing is lost. Its stock is moved to No location, and its products are left without that location. Restoring the location from the Trash links the products back, but the stock stays under No location until you move it back.

Lots, expiry dates & serial numbers

For food, medicine, cosmetics, electronics — anything that expires or has a serial number.

How do I record a lot, expiry date or serial numbers?

When stock comes in, open the Lot, expiry, serial numbers section on the Stock in sheet. Fill in what you need: Lot code, Expiry date, Supplier, Cost per unit, Serial numbers. Leave it closed for ordinary stock.

For serial numbers, type one per line, one per unit: the number of lines must equal the quantity. If you fill these fields on most deliveries, the section opens by itself.

Which lot is used first when stock goes out?

By default, the earliest expiry date first, then the oldest delivery. You can always pick a specific lot in Stock out from.

How do I see what is about to expire?

The Dashboard shows an Expiring soon card (expired, or within 30 days) whenever there is one. The Expiry dates report lists every lot with its days left, earliest first.

How is stock value calculated?

Each delivery keeps the cost it came in at. In Settings → Stock movements → Value stock at choose one of: Average cost of stock on hand, Latest receipt cost, or The product's cost price. This is used by the Inventory valuation report and the stock value on the product page.

Barcode scanner

Using the phone’s camera to find products, book stock and check prices.

How do I scan a barcode?

Open Scanner and point the camera at the code. If a product has that barcode, its stock sheet opens: choose Stock in or Stock out and the quantity. The flashlight button helps in dark places.

It says “Unknown barcode”.

No product has that barcode yet. Tap Add product with this barcode to create one with the code already filled in, or Search products for this code to attach it to an existing product.

If the scanned code matches a product’s SKU instead, the app says “Found by SKU — no barcode saved for this product yet.” and offers to save the code as its barcode.

Can I scan many items in a row (a delivery)?

Yes: turn on Batch on in the scanner. Each scan adds the product to a list; scanning the same product again adds one more. Then save the whole list as Stock in or Stock out in one go. Unknown codes are listed separately and skipped.

How do I check a price by scanning?

Turn on the Price check chip in the scanner. Each scan shows the selling price in large type, then cost and profit, stock value, cost by batch and recent sales. The camera keeps scanning, so you can check one item after another. The eye icon hides costs and profit when a customer can see the screen.

Which barcodes are supported? It scans slowly or reads wrong codes.

EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code-128, Code-39, Code-93, QR code and Data Matrix. In Settings → Barcode formats, switch off the ones you never use. Fewer formats scan faster and make fewer false reads. Beep and vibration are under Settings → Scan feedback.

The scanner says it needs the camera.

Tap Allow camera. If you refused before, Android won’t ask again: tap Open app settings and allow the camera under Permissions. The camera is only used while the scanner (or a photo) is open, and images are processed on the phone.

Can I use a handheld (USB / Bluetooth / PDA) barcode scanner?

Yes, through Remote access: open the app in the device’s browser and use the Scanner page or the handheld view, which reads the codes your scanner types. See handheld scanners.

Stocktake (counting stock)

Count what is really on the shelves and correct the app in one step.

How do I do a stocktake?
  1. Open Stocktake from the ⋮ menu.
  2. Choose where you are counting under Counting at (or All locations).
  3. For each product, swipe its counter up or down, tap it to type the exact count, or scan items to tally them. Leave a product blank to skip it.
  4. Tap Apply stock take. Only the products you changed are adjusted, and each difference is recorded as an adjustment.

A product held as several lots is counted lot by lot. Sales made while you count are taken into account, so they are not counted twice.

Tap Apply stock take before you leave the screen. Counts that haven’t been applied are not kept.
Can the app remind me to count?

Yes: Settings → Stock check reminder → Remind me to do a stock check. Choose Every day, Every week (on a weekday) or Every month (on a date), and a time.

Selling & bills

A simple till: sell, take the stock out and print or share a bill.

How do I sell and make a bill?
  1. Open Sell from the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap products, or scan their barcodes, to add them to the cart. Change quantities, prices and line discounts in the cart.
  3. Tap Checkout. Optionally add the customer’s name and phone, the payment method (Cash, Card, Bank transfer, Mobile wallet, On credit), a discount, tax, and the amount received. The change is worked out for you.
  4. Tap Complete sale, then print or share the bill.

The stock is taken out at the same moment, and the sale appears in Transactions and in the Sales & margin report. If any item doesn’t have enough stock, nothing is sold and the app tells you which one.

Where are my past bills? Can I reprint one?

On the Sell screen, tap Bills at the top. Search by bill number, customer or phone, filter by Today, 7 days, 30 days or All, then open a bill to print or share it again. Scanning the barcode printed on a bill opens it.

How do I cancel a sale or handle a return?

Open the bill in Bills and tap Void bill. Every item goes back into stock, and the bill is kept, marked VOID, so its number can still be found. Voiding cancels the whole bill: for a partial return, void it and make a new sale, or record a Stock in for the returned item.

How do I change how the bill looks (shop name, logo, paper size)?

On the Sell screen, tap Bill design. Pick a default design: Receipt 58 mm or Receipt 80 mm for thermal receipt printers, Half page (A5) or Invoice (A4) for ordinary printers. Then set your shop name, address and contact lines, tax or registration number, title, footer text, tax name and rate, number prefix and next bill number. The preview is the real bill. Print as soon as a sale is saved skips a step at the counter.

How do I print to a thermal / Bluetooth / USB receipt printer?

Bills print through Android’s print dialog, so any printer with an Android print service works. Install your printer’s print service app (or a universal one) from Google Play, choose the 58 mm or 80 mm design, and pick the printer in the print dialog. Without a printer you can always save as PDF and share it.

Is the bill a legal tax invoice?

The bill can show your tax or registration number and tax lines, but it is not certified for any country’s official tax or e-invoicing system. Check what your local rules require.

History & corrections

Every change of stock is recorded, and every mistake can be undone.

Where do I see all stock movements?

Transactions lists every movement: stock in, stock out, adjustments and transfers, with who made it, why, and the stock before and after. Filter by date range and type, or search by product, SKU or reason. Each product’s page also shows its own activity.

How do I correct or undo a movement?
  • Undo it completely: tap it in Transactions (or in the product’s activity) and choose Revert. The stock goes back as it was and the movement goes to the Trash.
  • Correct it in place: in the product’s activity, tap the movement and choose Edit to fix the amount, reason, lot, supplier, expiry or cost. It keeps its date and is marked as edited.
  • Just did it in Quick stock? Tap the undo arrow on the tile.

A revert or edit is refused when it would leave stock below zero (for example, the delivery you want to undo has already been sold).

Reports, exports & labels

Getting your data out as CSV or PDF, and printing labels.

How do I export a report (Excel / PDF)?
  1. Open Reports.
  2. Set the scope: a category for inventory reports, a date range for movement reports. Each report shows how many rows it will hold.
  3. Tap a report, then CSV (opens in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice) or PDF.

Can I put my logo and company name on PDF reports?

Yes. In Reports, tap Report design at the top (also in Settings → PDF Report). Set paper size, orientation, margins, logo, company name, fonts, table style, footer, page numbers and an accent colour, or start from one of four presets. The preview is the real PDF.

How do I print barcode or price labels?
  1. Open Labels from the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Presets and add a ready-made design (Retail price tag, Shelf edge label, Small barcode sticker, Thermal roll label, QR asset tag…), or create your own with New label: size, layout, font, fields and barcode type.
  3. Tap Print, select the products, and print. All the labels come out as one PDF.

For one product, open it and tap Print label at the top. Printing to a physical printer needs a print service (your printer maker’s app, or Mopria) from Google Play; otherwise save as PDF.

Import from a spreadsheet

Bringing in products, categories, suppliers or locations from a CSV file.

How do I import my products from Excel or Google Sheets?
  1. In Excel or Google Sheets, save or download your sheet as CSV. The app reads CSV files, not .xlsx.
  2. Open Import from the ⋮ menu and choose what you are importing (Products, Categories, Suppliers or Locations).
  3. Tap Pick CSV file. Not sure about the columns? Tap Save a template CSV, fill it in and pick that.
  4. Review the preview. Every row is marked NEW or UPDATE, with any warnings and rejected rows explained. Choose your options, then tap Import.

Nothing is written until you confirm.

What columns does the file need?

For products only sku and name are required. Others you can include: description, category, location, quantity, unit, min, max, cost, price, barcode, supplier, image. The first row must name the columns. Common spellings are understood (qty, stock, unit price…), and the Import screen lists every accepted name. Example:

sku,name,category,quantity,unit,cost,price,barcode
A-100,Widget,Hardware,25,units,4.50,9.99,4006381333931

Commas, semicolons or tabs, decimal commas, currency signs and most date formats are all handled. When a value has to be guessed, the preview says how it was read.

Will importing overwrite products I already have?

Only if you want it to. Products are matched by SKU. With Update records that already exist on, a matching product gets the values in your file, and only the columns your file contains. A file with just sku,name,quantity won’t erase prices or categories. Turn it off to add new products only.

If a product’s stock is spread over several locations or lots, the file can’t say where a new quantity belongs, so change that product’s stock from its page instead.

How do I get my whole product list into Excel?

Export the Product catalogue or Stock on hand report as CSV (see reports). The CSV can also be imported back into the app.

Backup, restore & changing phones

Your data lives on the phone. A backup elsewhere is what saves it if the phone is lost or reset.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all your data. Set up a backup before you do either.
How do I back up my data?
  1. Open Backup from the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Add destination and choose where backups go: Folder on this device (internal storage, SD card, USB drive, or a folder your cloud drive app syncs), FTP server, or Shared folder (SMB) (a NAS or a shared folder on a Windows PC).
  3. For a server, enter the address and sign-in, then Test connection. It checks that a file can really be written there.
  4. Tap Back up now, and turn on Automatic backups to save a copy about once a day.

Every backup goes to all your destinations. If one stops working, you get a notification and a Dashboard banner until it is fixed.

How do I restore a backup?

In Backup, find the backup in the saved list and tap Restore. You choose which data to bring back and see what it contains before anything changes. Restoring replaces your current data with the backup’s.

“This backup was made by a newer version of the app. Update the app, then restore it.” means the phone needs the latest version from Google Play first.

How do I move my data to a new phone?
  1. On the old phone, back up to somewhere the new phone can reach: an FTP/SMB server, a cloud-synced folder, or a file you copy across.
  2. On the new phone, install Stock Management. On the welcome screen choose Restore a backup, then From a file, From a server or From a folder.

Server passwords are not saved inside backups, for your security. Enter them again on the new phone.

Can two phones share the same stock?

The app has no online account, so two phones don’t sync with each other. To have several people work on the same stock, keep it on one phone and let the others use it through Remote access from a browser on the same Wi-Fi. A backup restored on another phone is a copy; changes on one won’t appear on the other.

Use it from a PC, tablet or handheld (Remote access)

Other devices on the same Wi-Fi open your stock in a web browser, with their own sign-ins and permissions.

How do I open the app on my computer?
  1. On the phone, open Remote access from the ⋮ menu. If the phone has a screen lock, confirm it.
  2. Tap Start server. The screen shows an address such as http://192.168.1.24:8080 and a QR code.
  3. Tap Add someone and create a sign-in: a name, a user name and a password.
  4. Tap Choose on that person and tick the screens and actions they get (or a preset: Look only, Stocktaker, Price checker, Everything). Nothing is allowed until you tick it.
  5. On the computer, type the address into a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and sign in.
  6. When you are finished, tap Stop server.

Every change made in the browser lands on the phone under that person’s name. The server never starts by itself.

The browser can’t open the address.
  • Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi (or the computer joined to the phone’s hotspot). Mobile data won’t work.
  • Guest or public Wi-Fi networks often stop devices from seeing each other. Use your main network or the phone’s hotspot.
  • Type the whole address including http:// and the port (:8080).
  • Check that the server is still running. Swiping the app away from the recent apps screen stops it, and so does a change of network.
  • The name address (ending in .local) only works if your router supports it. Use the number address instead.
  • Allow the app’s notifications. While the server runs, its notification holds the Stop button.
What can people do from the browser?

Whatever you allow: Dashboard, Products (view, add, edit), Scanner, Quick stock, Stocktake, History, Reports (CSV/PDF), Categories, Suppliers, Locations, moving stock between locations, printing labels, and Price check. Backup, import, the Trash, label design, settings, selling and Remote access itself stay on the phone. Nothing can be deleted from a browser.

Each browser can choose its own light or dark mode, theme, text size and list view under Display.

Using a handheld (PDA) or USB barcode scanner

Open the address in the device’s browser and sign in with an account allowed to use the Scanner. Tap the handheld icon at the top, choose Receiving or Dispatch, and scan. Each code is added to a list (scan again to add one more), and one button saves the whole run. Turn on Read scanner keys anywhere so the on-screen keyboard stays out of the way.

Unsaved lists are kept on the device if the Wi-Fi drops, so you can save them when you’re back. A browser can’t use the device’s camera to scan (it needs a secure connection), so use a scanner or type the code.

Can customers check prices themselves on a screen?

Yes. Create a sign-in with the Price checker preset, open Price check on a counter PC or tablet with a scanner, and choose Price checker screen. It shows your logo and company name (from the report design) and offers a full-screen button. What it shows and when it clears are set in Settings → Price checker screen.

Is Remote access safe?
  • It only works on private networks (home or office Wi-Fi, hotspot), never on the open internet.
  • Each person has their own password and only sees what you ticked. You can see who is connected, disconnect them, switch an account off or reset its password, and read the recent activity.
  • The connection is not encrypted, so use it on a network you trust, not on public Wi-Fi.
  • If the phone has no screen lock, anyone holding it can open Remote access. Adding a screen lock to the phone protects it.

Notifications

Low stock alerts, stock check reminders and backup warnings.

How do low stock alerts work? Can I choose the time?

Once a day the app sends one summary of the products below their minimum. Pick the time in Settings → Notifications → Low stock alert. It arrives around that time, because Android may delay it by a few minutes to save battery.

I don’t get any notifications.
  • Check that Settings → Notifications → Enable Notifications is on.
  • Allow notifications for the app: tap Turn on alerts on the Dashboard card, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Stock Management → Notifications.
  • Each type (Low stock alerts, Stock check reminders, Backup problems, Remote access) can be switched on or off separately in Android’s notification settings for the app.
  • Some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung…) stop background apps to save battery. Allow the app to run in the background, or set its battery use to “no restrictions”.

Trash

Nothing you delete is gone for good until you empty it.

How do I get back something I deleted?

Open Trash from the ⋮ menu. Deleted products, categories, suppliers, locations, labels and reverted movements are all there. Tap Restore: the app first tells you exactly what will happen. Restoring only adds back; it never overwrites anything you have now (a clashing SKU or name is given a new one, such as A-100-2).

Delete forever removes one entry; Empty clears the whole Trash. Neither affects your current data.

Problems & contact

A feature on this page is missing from my app.

Update Stock Management from Google Play. Recent updates added moving stock between locations, lots and serial numbers, selling and bills, price check and more. Your data is kept when you update.

Where is my data stored? Who can see it?

Only on your phone, in the app’s private storage. We don’t receive your products, stock or sales. Read the privacy policy.

How do I ask a question or report a problem?

In the app: Settings → Send feedback (or the speech bubble at the top of the main screens). Choose the type, write your message, and put your email in the Contact (optional) field if you’d like an answer. Without it we can read your message but can’t reply. Sending needs an internet connection.
